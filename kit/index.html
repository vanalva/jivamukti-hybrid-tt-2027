Good to know

  1. Stories and reels are 1080 × 1920. Keep text and logos out of the top 250 px and the bottom 340 px, where Instagram puts its buttons.
  2. Most elements have light text, so place their transparent PNGs on dark footage or on the charcoal colour.
  3. The QR code opens hybrid.jivamuktiyoga.com/us-2027
  4. To edit a template in Canva, choose Create a design, then Import file, and pick the .pptx. Text, shapes and photos stay editable; swap the photos for your own footage.
