Hybrid TT US 2027 - Asset kit
=============================

Graphic elements of the Hybrid 300hr TT US 2027 flyers, poster and banners, to build stories, reels and posts on any platform.
Every file is cut from the final print files, so it matches what was printed.

FOLDERS
01 Logos and title       300hr Teacher Training lockup, cobra, JIVAMUKTI YOGA logotype, QR code, title (3 lines, 1 line), dates, title + dates.
                         Each in Orange, Charcoal, Light grey and White, on transparent and on the brand colours, plus versions
                         "on-light" / "on-dark". SVG and PDF (vector, any size) and PNG (1024 and 2048 px). See contact-sheet.pdf.
02 Elements              Buttons, Apply now / Find out more pills, QR with "Apply now", the five stat boxes, the five reasons,
                         location, deposit, modules, "Who is this training for?" and testimonial cards, faculty cards (residential
                         and online), headings, text blocks, footer bar. Transparent or on charcoal, SVG plus PNG 1080 and 2160 px wide.
                         See contact-sheet.pdf.
03 Social backgrounds    The title and the lockup centred on each brand colour at 1080x1080, 1080x1350, 1080x1920 and 1200x630.
04 Photos                Class, location, residential and online faculty photos.
05 Fonts                 Montserrat (SIL Open Font License, free to install). Also on Google Fonts.

TIPS
- Over video or photos use the transparent PNGs; for Canva or Illustrator use the SVGs (they scale without losing quality).
- Instagram/Facebook: stories and reels 1080x1920, feed posts 1080x1350 or 1080x1080, link previews 1200x630.
- Keep text and logos out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px of a story or reel (Instagram covers them).
- The QR code opens https://hybrid.jivamuktiyoga.com/us-2027/
